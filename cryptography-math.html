RSA Algorithm

Key generation · encryption · decryption · digital signature

Press Play or Step to begin
0.55s
How: n = p·q, φ = (p−1)(q−1); pick e coprime to φ, then d = e⁻¹ mod φ so that m^(e·d) ≡ m (mod n). Anyone can encrypt with (e, n) — only d decrypts. Signing flips it: s = m^d, verify with e. Security rests on factoring n being hard. Keygen O(log²n) | encrypt/decrypt = modular exponentiation
