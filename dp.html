Longest Increasing Subsequence

Find the longest strictly increasing subsequence

Press Play or Step to begin
0.5s
How: For each element, check all previous elements. If arr[j] < arr[i], update dp[i] = max(dp[i], dp[j]+1). The answer is max of all dp[i]. Time: O(n²) | Space: O(n)

Matrix Chain Multiplication

Find optimal parenthesization to minimize multiplications

Press Play or Step to begin
0.4s
How: For each chain length, try all split points. dp[i][j] = min over k of (dp[i][k]+dp[k+1][j]+p[i-1]*p[k]*p[j]). Time: O(n³) | Space: O(n²)

Bellman-Ford Algorithm

Shortest paths with negative edges & negative cycle detection

Press Play or Step to begin
0.7s
How: Relax all edges V-1 times. If any distance still updates on the V-th pass, a negative cycle exists. Time: O(VE) | Space: O(V)

Floyd-Warshall Algorithm

All-pairs shortest paths via intermediate vertices

Press Play or Step to begin
0.8s
How: For each intermediate vertex k, check if going through k gives shorter paths: dist[i][j] = min(dist[i][j], dist[i][k]+dist[k][j]). Time: O(V³) | Space: O(V²)

Kadane's Algorithm

Maximum contiguous subarray sum in linear time

Press Play or Step to begin
0.6s
How: At each position, either extend the current subarray or start fresh. current = max(arr[i], current+arr[i]). Track the global max. Time: O(n) | Space: O(1)
